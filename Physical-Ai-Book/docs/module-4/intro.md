# Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models

Welcome to Module 4 of the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Book. This module focuses on Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, covering fundamentals, advanced applications, and evaluation and ethics of these powerful multimodal AI systems.

## Overview

In this module, you will explore:
- VLA Fundamentals
- Advanced VLA Applications
- VLA Evaluation and Ethics

Each topic includes multiple lessons that provide both theoretical knowledge and practical applications in the context of Vision-Language-Action models for physical AI systems.

## Prerequisites

Before diving into this module, you should have a comprehensive understanding of:
- Fundamentals of physical AI (covered in Module 1)
- Perception and reasoning in physical systems (covered in Module 2)
- Advanced physical AI applications (covered in Module 3)
- Deep learning and multimodal AI concepts

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Understand and implement Vision-Language-Action models
- Apply VLA models to complex physical AI tasks
- Evaluate VLA models and consider ethical implications in physical AI systems
