# Content Guidelines for Physical AI Book

## 1. Progressive Learning

Each lesson builds on previous concepts while introducing new material. Ensure that concepts are introduced in a logical sequence that supports learning progression from beginner to advanced levels.

## 2. Hands-on Focus

Every lesson must include practical exercises, simulations, or real-world implementations. Theoretical knowledge must be validated through experimentation and observation of physical systems.

## 3. Multiple Learning Styles

Content must accommodate:
- Visual learners: Include diagrams, charts, and visual representations
- Auditory learners: Provide clear explanations and discussions
- Kinesthetic learners: Include hands-on activities and practical exercises

## 4. Accessibility Standards

All content must meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards:
- Use proper heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3, etc.)
- Include alternative text for images
- Ensure sufficient color contrast
- Provide captions for multimedia content
- Use semantic HTML elements

## 5. Cultural Sensitivity

Examples and applications should consider diverse perspectives and avoid cultural bias.

## 6. Quality Standards

Content must be:
- Accurate and well-researched
- Properly cited with references
- Clear and concise
- Technically correct
- Well-structured and organized

## 7. Physical AI Focus

All content must emphasize the embodied nature of Physical AI systems that perceive, reason, and act in the physical world, covering the integration of sensors, actuators, edge computing, and AI-driven control systems.

## 8. Docusaurus Compatibility

Content must be structured to work properly with Docusaurus, including:
- Proper Markdown formatting
- Correct frontmatter metadata
- Appropriate linking between related content
- Responsive design considerations
