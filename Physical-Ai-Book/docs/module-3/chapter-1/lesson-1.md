# Communication Modalities

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Understand different communication modalities for human-robot interaction
- Design multimodal communication interfaces
- Analyze the strengths and limitations of different modalities

## Prerequisites

Before starting this lesson, you should:
- Understand basic concepts of human-computer interaction
- Have knowledge of sensor and actuator systems
- Be familiar with basic signal processing concepts

## Introduction

Communication modalities form the foundation of human-robot interaction in Physical AI systems. Effective communication requires understanding and utilizing various channels through which humans and robots can exchange information, including voice, gesture, visual displays, and other sensory modalities.

## Summary

Effective communication modalities are essential for successful human-robot interaction in Physical AI systems. Understanding and appropriately integrating multiple communication channels enables more natural and intuitive interaction between humans and embodied AI systems.
