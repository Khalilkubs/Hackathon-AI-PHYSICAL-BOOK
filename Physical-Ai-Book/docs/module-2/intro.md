# Module 2: Perception and Reasoning in Physical Systems

Welcome to Module 2 of the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Book. This module focuses on perception and reasoning in physical systems, covering essential topics such as computer vision for physical AI, spatial reasoning and navigation, and decision making and planning.

## Overview

In this module, you will explore:
- Computer Vision for Physical AI
- Spatial Reasoning and Navigation
- Decision Making and Planning

Each topic includes multiple lessons that provide both theoretical knowledge and practical applications in the context of physical AI systems.

## Prerequisites

Before diving into this module, you should have a basic understanding of:
- Fundamentals of physical AI (covered in Module 1)
- Basic programming concepts
- Mathematics fundamentals for robotics

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:
- Understand and implement computer vision techniques for physical AI
- Apply spatial reasoning and navigation algorithms
- Design decision-making and planning systems for physical environments
