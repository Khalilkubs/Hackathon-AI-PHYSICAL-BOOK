# Ethical Considerations

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Identify key ethical challenges in Physical AI systems
- Apply ethical frameworks to design decisions
- Implement responsible deployment practices for interactive AI systems

## Prerequisites

Before starting this lesson, you should:
- Understand basic concepts of ethics and moral philosophy
- Have knowledge of human-robot interaction principles
- Be familiar with privacy and security concepts

## Introduction

Ethical considerations are paramount in the development and deployment of Physical AI systems that interact with humans. These embodied systems have the potential to significantly impact human welfare, autonomy, and dignity, requiring careful consideration of moral implications throughout their lifecycle.
