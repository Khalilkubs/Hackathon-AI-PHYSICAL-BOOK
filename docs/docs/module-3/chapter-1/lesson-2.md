# Social Intelligence

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Understand the principles of social intelligence in Physical AI systems
- Implement basic social cognition mechanisms
- Design socially appropriate behaviors for robots

## Prerequisites

Before starting this lesson, you should:
- Understand basic concepts of human psychology and behavior
- Have knowledge of perception and reasoning systems
- Be familiar with basic machine learning concepts

## Introduction

Social intelligence in Physical AI systems encompasses the ability to perceive, understand, and appropriately respond to human social behavior. This capability is essential for robots that interact with humans in social contexts, requiring understanding of social norms, emotions, and interpersonal dynamics.
