# Deployment Strategies

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Design deployment strategies for Physical AI systems
- Plan for safe and effective system rollout
- Implement operational procedures for sustained deployment

## Prerequisites

Before starting this lesson, you should:
- Understand system testing and validation concepts
- Have knowledge of system integration principles
- Be familiar with operational and maintenance procedures

## Introduction

Deployment strategies determine how Physical AI systems transition from development to operational use. This process involves careful planning to ensure safe, reliable, and effective operation in real-world environments while maintaining system performance and user satisfaction.

## Summary

Effective deployment strategies are essential for successfully transitioning Physical AI systems from development to operational use. A comprehensive approach including phased rollout, safety measures, user training, and ongoing support ensures successful and sustainable deployment.
